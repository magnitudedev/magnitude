# Hardware photographs

The bundled inventory contains 33 photographs, selected by explicit device identity or accelerator name. `inventory.json` is the canonical attribution and matching inventory. The desktop validates it with Effect Schema and resolves its files through Vite so every photograph works offline.

Photos identify a device family, not the user's finish or internal configuration. A GPU image is a reference card, not a picture of the user's complete computer. Exact variant suffixes are significant: Ti, SUPER, XT, XTX, D and Laptop are not interchangeable. Unknown identities remain unnamed by the inventory rather than receiving a guessed enclosure.

Apple identifiers are verified against Apple's identification pages: [MacBook Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108052), [MacBook Air](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102869), [Mac mini](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102852), [Mac Studio](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102231), and [iMac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108054). Surface model names follow Microsoft's [System Model reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/surface/surface-system-sku-reference).

Mac Pro tower and rack variants share identifiers, so a tower photograph is not selected from that identifier alone. Dell's reused model numbers and ambiguous Framework identifiers also require more evidence before adding enclosure matches.

New additions use Wikimedia's high-resolution thumbnails without local alteration. Source pages and licenses remain accessible from the photo credit in the app. Shared enclosures may use the pictured generation as family reference, with actual processor information displayed independently from engine observations.
