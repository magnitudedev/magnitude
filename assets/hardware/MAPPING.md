## Source and quality records

The machine-readable [inventory](inventory.json) retains every product page, original image URL, and additional firmware-identity evidence. [Photo audit](photo-audit.json) records dimensions, real transparency and SHA-256 hashes. Run `python assets/hardware/tools/audit.py` with Pillow to regenerate this document and validate every asset.

GPU matching removes only the NVIDIA / AMD / Intel and GeForce / Radeon prefixes. Laptop GPU, Ti, SUPER, XT, XTX and regional suffixes remain significant. Lenovo product-version matching never falls back to a similarly named family. The reused Dell XPS 15 9530 identifier additionally requires a verified 2023 processor token.
