Local coding normally means finding a model, choosing a quantization, installing and tuning a runtime, connecting it to an agent, and keeping the stack working. Magnitude handles every layer of the local stack so you can focus on coding.

Magnitude is open source under the [Apache 2.0 license](https://github.com/magnitudedev/magnitude/blob/main/LICENSE).
