Magnitude runs models from Hugging Face: the models Magnitude recommends for your machine, any quant or fine-tune of a supported model family, and GGUF files already on your computer.

## Recommendations

Magnitude measures how models run on your machine and recommends the ones that run well here. You see them when you first open Magnitude and on **Models** until you have downloaded a model. Use **Only models from US developers** to narrow the list.

Open a recommendation to compare its quants: each shows its estimated speed, its size, and how much of the model fits in GPU memory, in RAM or is streamed from your SSD. **Best** marks the quant Magnitude chose. A smaller quant is faster and needs less memory; a larger one is closer to the original model. Speeds are estimates for a long agent conversation, not a benchmark of your task.

## Explore Hugging Face

On **Models**, click **Explore models**, then search Hugging Face or paste a link to a model. Results only include models built on a family Magnitude runs; a quant that doesn't fit your machine can't be downloaded. **What runs in Magnitude** lists the supported model families; any quant or fine-tune of them runs too.

Open a result to choose a quant and a drafter, then click **Download**. For a gated model, sign in to Hugging Face from the prompt, accept the model's terms on Hugging Face, then try again.

## Models already on your computer

Magnitude finds GGUF models you downloaded with other apps, such as LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, or the Hugging Face cache, and runs them in place without copying them. To choose which folders it looks in, open **Settings → Storage → Model locations**. A file Magnitude can't run is listed under **Doesn't run in Magnitude yet**.

## Your models

**Models** lists every model on your computer, with its download progress or state.

- **Load** loads a model into memory, and **Stop** unloads it without deleting it. An agent's request also loads the model it asks for. Magnitude unloads a model after an hour without requests, or when the system runs short of memory.
- **⋯ → Explore other quants** shows the model's other quants.
- **⋯ → Delete…** deletes a downloaded model. For a model found in another app's folder, **Hide** removes it from the list without deleting the file.

Open a model to see where it runs, its source, where it's stored, its drafter, and its developer and license. Model licenses are separate from Magnitude's.

Downloaded models are kept in `~/.magnitude/models`, or `%USERPROFILE%\.magnitude\models` on Windows. To use another folder, choose it under **Settings → Storage → Model storage**; Magnitude shows the command that moves your existing models.

## Drafters

Magnitude speeds up generation with speculative decoding: a small drafter model drafts tokens ahead and the main model checks them. Recommended models come with a drafter. For other models Magnitude chooses one automatically; to change it, open the model and click **Change…**, or choose **None**.

## From the terminal

```sh
magnitude models recommend
magnitude models search "QUERY"
magnitude models pull "MODEL_ID"
magnitude models list
magnitude models load "MODEL_ID"
```

`pull` takes a recommended model's ID, a Hugging Face `owner/repo` with an optional `:QUANT`, or a link. See [CLI reference](/reference) for every command.

## Change your agent's model

Choose a downloaded model in your agent's model selector. If it isn't listed, refresh the agent with **Refresh connection** in **Connections** or `magnitude connections sync`, then reload or restart it as described in its [integration guide](/integrations/overview).
